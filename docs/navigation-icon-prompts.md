Crisp softly rounded edges and strong medium-thick geometry. Almost flat with only subtle shallow extrusion to clarify planes. White-dominant. Designed to read at 32 pixels. NO glossy glass, NO chrome, NO realistic medical anatomy, NO texture, NO glow, NO decorative details, NO app tile, NO rounded-square container, NO button border, NO words, letters, numerals or watermark. No checkerboard painted in the picture: genuine transparent background. Single isolated icon only.
